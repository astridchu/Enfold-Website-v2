The Enfold Vessel

An environment designed for going inward.

A vessel designed for mind-body restoration.

The vessel brings light, sound and vibration together through a warm water surface, creating one immersive experience you feel through the whole body.

The science beneath the experience.

Synchronised light pulses gently stimulate brainwave states conducive to mental relaxation and restoration.

Light moves with the journey. A synchronised light system is experienced through closed eyes, adding another sensory layer to the sound and vibration. Rather than asking for your attention, the elements are designed to work together – one environment, one experience.

Guided and unguided outcome-based sound programs developed with musicians, scientists, and therapists.

Not only heard. Felt. Built-in speakers (or headphones) create a spacious sound field around the body, while deeper frequencies move through the water surface as vibration. Music becomes part of the environment – surrounding you, moving through you, and shaping the arc of the session.

Low frequency sound vibrations through heated water support blood circulation, muscle relaxation, and nervous system regulation.

Sound becomes something you can feel. Low-frequency vibration travels through a temperature-controlled water surface, distributing warmth and sensation evenly beneath the body. You remain fully clothed while the surface creates the softness and support of floating – an environment designed to make letting go feel a little easier.

Measurement of brain and heart activity via a brain and heart sensing headband provides insight into each session and adapts the session in real-time.

Each individual is unique. The integration of the experience is just as important as the experience itself. Measured brain and heart data combined with subjective insights help you navigate your inner world. Enfold learns as you learn and the relationship deepens over time.

Frequently asked questions

What does a session feel like?

You lie down, fully clothed, on a warm waterbed and close your eyes. Light moves behind your eyelids, sound surrounds you, and its deeper notes reach you as gentle vibration through the water. Sessions last 25 to 50 minutes.

How is the vessel looked after?

The surface can be wiped down with a cleaning product between sessions, and the pillow slips are washable. Once a month, a water conditioning tablet goes into the waterbed. The water itself never needs changing.

Is the vessel right for everyone?

The vessel isn’t suitable during pregnancy, for anyone with a pacemaker or epilepsy, or for people who are sensitive to light or sound.

Where are vessels made, and how do they ship?

We make every vessel in our own workshop in Bali. Studios of eight or more are shipped directly from there; single vessels may be available through local retailers.

What makes a space an Enfold studio?

An Enfold studio is a dedicated space with at least eight vessels, fitted out and run to our standards under the Enfold name, through a licence or franchise.

How much does a vessel cost?

It depends on how you’ll use it: as a studio, or as a single vessel in your space. Request the product brochure and we’ll share current pricing for your region.

What should I charge for a session?

As a guide, studios charge around $1 a minute for group sessions, and around $2 a minute for private ones, depending on the market.
