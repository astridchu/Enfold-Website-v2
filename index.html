It started with a question.

Enfold began with burnout. A period of profound change led our founder, Laurent, to Bali, where he explored sound, stillness and the body.

What helped wasn’t a technique. It was the feeling of being held by the right conditions.

One question followed: what if those conditions could be created on purpose, and made part of everyday life? That question became Enfold.

The vessel is just the beginning.

Through studios, practices and the experiences still to come, we want to make rest and reconnection part of everyday life.

Frequently asked questions

What happens in a session?

You lie down on a warm waterbed and close your eyes. Light moves behind your eyelids, sound surrounds you, and its deeper notes reach you as gentle vibration through the water. There is nothing to do but rest.

What should I wear?

Whatever you’re comfortable in. You stay fully clothed for the whole session.

How long is a session?

Between 25 and 50 minutes, depending on the journey you choose.

Is it right for everyone?

The vessel isn’t suitable during pregnancy, for anyone with a pacemaker or epilepsy, or for people who are sensitive to light or sound. If you’re unsure, ask your doctor before you book.

Is it a medical treatment?

No. Enfold is a space to rest and turn inward. It isn’t a medical treatment and doesn’t replace one.

Where can I try it?

In Enfold studios and partner spaces in Bali, Singapore, Sydney, Las Vegas and beyond. Find a location.
